Due dates are estimates

We’re a small, hands-on business and we often have a high volume of orders. The due date on your docket is our best estimate, not a guarantee, and it may change without notice.

We’ll always try to let you know if your item will take longer, but we can’t promise to reach you every time.

If a delay doesn’t suit you

You’re welcome to collect your item free of charge at any time before work on it has begun.

Once work has started

If your item has already been dismantled, or the repair or making process has begun, a part or full fee may be charged before we can return it.

Your agreement

Leaving your item with us means you accept these terms. If you’re not happy with them, please collect your item as soon as possible.
